Step 4 (note 1): the globe follows the finger. File: WorldMap.tsx.
- Turntable with north up:
  - Module-level VIEW = { yaw, pitch }. Each frame, M = mul(axisRot([1,0,0], pitch), axisRot([0,1,0], yaw)).
  - θf = asin(focusDir()[1]), about 27°. The latitude at the focus is latF = θf + pitch.
  - Home is the centre of her islands: yaw = −(mean lon), pitch = (mean lat) − θf. This replaces today's fixed −2°.
- Drag, 1:1 at the focus:
  - yaw += dx / (R · max(0.6, cos latF)); pitch += dy / (R · focusDir()[2]). Today's gain is 0.55/R.
  - latF is limited to −32° … +38°, so every slot can reach the middle. Past a limit, apply 35% of the move. On release it eases back over 300 ms, ease-out, with no overshoot.
  - Ignore a second finger. Delete the pinch stub and its comment.
- Fling:
  - Keep {t: ev.timeStamp, x, y} samples for 100 ms, using getCoalescedEvents when present.
  - On release: no fling if the newest sample is older than 50 ms. Otherwise take the velocity over the last 80 ms (Δt ≥ 10 ms), cap the finger speed at 0.85 px/ms (about 2.1 rad/s) and convert it with the same gains.
- Glide, by elapsed time:
  - dt = clamp(now − last, 0, 32) ms; yaw += wYaw·dt; pitch += wPitch·dt; w *= 0.996^dt.
  - That is τ ≈ 250 ms: a hard flick glides at most about 30°, a gentle one 5–15°. Everything is still within about 1.2 s, the same at 60 and 120 Hz.
  - Stop below 8 px/s at the surface. Hitting a pitch limit sets wPitch to 0.
  - Reduce Motion: no glide, and the drag stays 1:1.
- A touch stops the glide. If the globe was moving faster than 50 px/s, that touch never counts as a tap.
- turnTo(lon, lat) and home():
  - turnTo targets yaw = −lon (the shortest way, wrapped to ±π) and pitch = lat − θf (clamped). home() targets Home.
  - Both animate over 350 ms, ease-out cubic; instant under Reduce Motion.
  - Delete the axis-angle spin code.
- Detent: only while she drags or the globe glides. When the island nearest the focus changes and is within 15° of it, call haptics.tick() and playWheelTick(), at most every 120 ms.
- No auto-settle and no idle spin. "Back to me" stays.
- Verify with synthetic events:
  - A 150 px drag at the focus moves the island under the finger 150 px ±5%.
  - Releasing right after the last move glides and stops within 1.2 s, at most 0.53 rad in total.
  - Resting 100 ms before releasing gives no glide.
  - Dragging 600 px down stops at the limit and eases back without overshoot.
  - After circular drags, north is still up.
