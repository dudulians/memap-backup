# MeMap — Apple App Enhancements nomination & editorial deck brief

**Prepared:** November 7, 2026  
**Planned release:** December 1, 2026 (subject to App Review)  
**Nomination type:** App Enhancements  
**Working nomination name:** MeMap — Your Days Become a World  
**Audience:** Apple App Store editorial team  
**Purpose:** Explain a meaningful product enhancement, not a generic rebrand, advertisement, or investor pitch.

## 1. Core editorial story

**Your days become a world you can explore.**

MeMap turns a small daily act—answering a few personal yes/no questions—into a growing archipelago. Its islands, paths, map and globe give a welcoming visual form to returning and observing over time. Behind the beauty are practical functions: a record of how often experiences occur, links showing observed associations (not causes), user-defined thresholds marked by lighthouses, and comparisons around small changes.

**Why it matters:** The update aims to make self-observation easier to return to, while keeping its actual analytical value. The game-like environment is not a substitute for insight: it gives those insights a place and a sense of progression.

**Positioning guardrail:** Don't pitch MeMap as a game that happens to track habits, or as an app that discovers medical causes. It is a private daily tracker made more engaging by a meaningful, evolving world.

## 2. App Store Connect — ready-to-use English copy

### Nomination name (max 60 characters)

**MeMap — Your Days Become a World**

### Nomination Description (limit 1,000 characters; current count 851)

What if a one-minute daily check-in became a world of your own?

MeMap is evolving from a private yes/no tracker into an explorable archipelago. Life areas become islands that grow as people return; a map and globe bring their daily journey to life, with gentle milestones and rewards.

The beauty has a purpose. MeMap helps people see how often experiences recur and which events tend to occur together or a day apart. Connections reflect observed associations, not causes. Users can set personal thresholds marked by lighthouses and compare their days before and during a two-week change.

This major update makes self-observation feel less like keeping records and more like building a world—without sacrificing clarity, control, or privacy. Check-ins take about a minute, need no account, and stay on the device.

Target release: December 1, 2026.

### Helpful Details (limit 500 characters; current count 380)

MeMap is designed for people who want to understand recurring experiences without turning reflection into another chore. It uses simple yes/no questions, never diagnoses or predicts bad days, and sends only gentle reminders. The evolving islands and optional sheep avatar make the experience warmer, while users retain control over their questions, thresholds, and on-device data.

## 3. Pitch deck plan for Claude — six editorial slides

**Format:** 6-page landscape PDF, 16:9; editable slide source if feasible. It should look like a polished App Store editorial feature, not a funding pitch and not a long instruction manual. Use real application screenshots captured from the current build. No made-up UI or made-up statistics. Soft, restrained MeMap palette. Clear English. One headline + a brief supporting line per slide. Layout should elevate actual screens rather than generic illustrations.

### Slide 1 — Cover / emotional hook

- **Headline:** Your days. A world of your own.
- **Subline:** MeMap turns one-minute daily check-ins into a growing personal archipelago.
- **Visual:** Full, authentic World map or globe, with islands and character visible, ample negative space for typography.
- **Intent:** Make an editor curious in under 3 seconds.

### Slide 2 — Why this is a major enhancement

- **Headline:** From tracking days to exploring them.
- **Subline:** A familiar daily ritual, reimagined as a living, interactive world.
- **Visual:** One clean **Before / After** comparison. Previous app screen on left; new World screen on right. Match crops/scale; no unfair edits.
- **Intent:** Establish substantive change, not just visual polish.

### Slide 3 — Growth / islands

- **Headline:** Every return brings your world to life.
- **Subline:** Each life area has an island that develops as the user checks in.
- **Visual:** The **same actual island asset** in 3 clearly separated growth stages, plus (optional) a real map screenshot showing multiple islands.
- **Intent:** Show reward and visual progression without clutter.

### Slide 4 — Data-driven links / real value

- **Headline:** The connections have meaning.
- **Subline:** Explore what tends to happen together—or a day apart. Associations, never claims of causation.
- **Visual:** Authentic map showing a dotted link, next to real Links comparison UI showing **both groups**. If example data is used, label clearly **Illustrative example**. Never present illustrative numbers as findings.
- **Intent:** Demonstrate why this is more than a decorative island builder.

### Slide 5 — The daily journey / mascot / rewards

- **Headline:** A small ritual worth returning to.
- **Subline:** A few yes/no answers, gentle milestones, and a world that keeps unfolding.
- **Visual:** Real check-in UI → actual path / milestones → real reward/collectible, if shipped. The sheep avatar may bridge the scenes, using the exact approved avatar; do not invent a new mascot.
- **Intent:** Show both low friction and emotional attachment.

### Slide 6 — Agency and privacy / close

- **Headline:** Your patterns. Your next steps.
- **Subline:** Set personal rules, explore changes over time, and keep your answers private on your phone.
- **Visual:** Real lighthouse/rule state alongside one real before/during two-week comparison; a subtle trust footer: **1 min/day · No account · On-device answers**.
- **Intent:** Leave editors with the useful, responsible purpose of the app and a credible product story.
