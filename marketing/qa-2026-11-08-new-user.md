## What worked well (keep it)
- **Counts match across all 8 days.** "Week 1 · k/7", the streak, "Your first week's report in N evenings", "First report — N days to go", and the calendar ("3 migraines") all agree with the week report ("Migraines: 3 of 7 days").
- **Every deck ends with what's next:** "Done! 9 answers saved. Your next step is waiting on the path. [Continue]".
- **Every light on the path opens a card.** Past days give "See or change answers". The note step offers a clear "Not today". Future lights say when they open.
- **The daytime sheet** "It's still daytime — Which day do you want to answer for?" (Yesterday / Tonight) works, and a past day's deck shows its date ("Sat, 7 Nov").
- **Topics by island:** islands open in place, "1 picked" counters, and the limit line stays pinned under the title while the list scrolls.
- **Questions screen:** "MAIN QUESTION" vs "ALSO TRACK THESE, TO SEE WHAT GOES TOGETHER", plus "Examples — change them anytime."
- **Colours follow meaning:** rose red for hard, green for good in calendars, link dots and the report; gold for done steps.
- **One font:** every screen I checked used only "MeMap Sans" (= Manrope).
- **EN calendars** have a weekday header and dates, and days are dots.
- **Safety:** "Something worrying?" sits on headache and migraine cards, "Need to talk?" on anxious and lonely cards. Advice stays general ("See a doctor and show your migraine days"). Drug and test names exist only in the RETIRED_ADVICE list.
- **Bubbles** show a "How it works" sheet before first use. **The hunch** says "MeMap will check them against your days" and when the result comes.
- **Most RU copy reads naturally and is gender-free:** welcome, onboarding, tour, «Ты сегодня молодец!». The problems are the template questions (B3).
