## What works well

- **Live language switch EN ↔ RU.** Today, World, all 4 cards, every sheet, island sheets, link sentences and rule cards switch at once, with no reload. Only the user's own notes and own plans stay in her words. The globe button and Settings → Language agree.
- **The old look keeps everything.** All 18 questions and every answer are still there. "Try the new MeMap" is the first row in the old Settings. Only the path weeks reset (B1).
- **Links list.** Real answer words, an "On other days" baseline, two dot rows with "Holds up", and "58 days of answers". Island tabs only show links that involve that island.
- **When to act cards.** The question, the rule, the plan, and hard counts in rose. "See details" has 30 day dots with a legend, plus Once / Every day for 2 weeks.
- **The test reminder works.** The switch and time change save at once (21:30 saved) and the row updates.
- **Support sheet ("Need to talk?").** Calm, short, with the right line for the time zone (800 4673; 998 / 999). Natural Russian, a link to the report, and only one disclaimer.
- **Report sheet and doctor short page.** Who for, short or full, period, questions by island with ticks and MAIN tags, a live preview, and "Nothing is sent anywhere until you share it". The page is clean, colours follow meaning, and calendars have Mo–Su with dates.
- **Month calendar.** Weekday letters, round dots, rose/green by meaning, a note marker, and the open day shown as a dashed ring. "Select several days" works.
- **Night theme.** Consistent: no white flashes, readable cards, a night globe and sky.
- **Advice stays general.** "See a GP and ask for a check-up", "See a doctor about the back pain": no named tests or drugs anywhere I looked.
- **What helps has its own sheet** (no empty "Week summary"). "See how it went" shows the result and the garden.
- **No JavaScript errors** in any run, apart from the expected `InAppReview` "not implemented on web".
