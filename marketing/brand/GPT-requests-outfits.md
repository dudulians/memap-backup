Uliana's idea (Nov 2 2026): the chest and progress also give **rewards for the character, such as new outfits and accessories**.

Nothing has been drawn yet. Before we start, please decide one thing.

## Choose one way

**A. Separate stickers worn on any avatar** (recommended to start)
- 8 items, one picture each, that MeMap puts on top of the chosen animal.
- Cheap: 8 pictures in total.
- Risk: on some animals a hat may sit a little off. I'll fit each one by hand for all 30.

**B. Ready-made avatars wearing the outfit**
- Each animal is drawn already wearing the item. It looks perfect, with nothing "stuck on".

## Prompt for option A (one item per request)

> Soft 3D cute app icon style, same style as the attached avatar set (no outlines, gentle lighting, pastel colours, matte). A single **[ITEM, e.g. explorer hat]** for a chibi animal character, front view, centered, made to sit on top of a round head about 300 px wide. Transparent background, PNG, 512×512, the item fills about 70% of the width. No character, no text, no shadow on the ground.

Attach 2–3 of your avatars as the style reference.

## Prompt for option B (one animal + one item)

> Take the attached avatar **[ANIMAL]** and draw the exact same character wearing **[ITEM]**. Keep the pose, face, colours, proportions and soft 3D style identical; only add the item, sitting naturally. Transparent background, PNG, 512×512, same framing as the original.
